# Evaluation

Generated by `python -m mem.evaluate` on 6 synthetic archives (seeds 300 to 305, 14,978 records) that the demo and the tests never use.
Every record is generated, so each step has a known right answer. The archives are far tidier than a real person's: read these as a check that the pipeline works, not as real-world accuracy.

## 1. Multi-hop questions

291 questions, one per restaurant dinner on a trip, in three phrasings, of the form *"Find the Italian restaurant from my 2024 Chicago trip where I photographed a red motorcycle, then show the receipt and who I was with."*
Each trip also contains photos sharing the colour or the object, and the same cities and cuisines recur across trips.

| | Right restaurant | Right receipt | Everyone present, nobody extra |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chain: trip, photo, moment, receipt, people | 93.8% | 92.8% | 79.4% |
| Baseline: plain vector search for the whole question, receipts only, top 1 | | 8.9% | |
| Same baseline, right receipt anywhere in the top 5 | | 33.3% | |

The chain declined to answer 10 of 291 questions rather than guess. People named: precision 0.997, recall 0.811.
The photo hop alone (best match for the described object across the whole archive, no trip filter) picked the right photo 48.5% of the time: the trip filter is what makes it reliable.

## 2. Grouping records into moments

Pairs of records placed in the same moment, against pairs generated by the same real-world event:

| | Precision | Recall | F1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Two-hour silence or 600 m jump | 0.969 | 0.850 | 0.905 |
| Baseline: same calendar day | 0.537 | 1.000 | 0.699 |

The misses are lunches that run straight into an afternoon of sightseeing, and dinners with nothing recorded between a message and the receipt.

## 3. Linking people

Mentions in calendar entries and messages linked to a contact card. Two contacts share a first name on purpose.

| | Precision | Recall | F1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rules, refusing to guess an ambiguous first name | 1.000 | 0.971 | 0.985 |
| Baseline: an ambiguous first name is the first such contact | 0.985 | 0.985 | 0.985 |

86 mentions were left unlinked because the first name fitted two contacts and nothing else in the moment settled it.

## 4. Restaurants and cuisine

897 restaurants were recovered from scanned receipts (merchant lines carry scanner errors such as `BELLAV1TA`); the cuisine vote was right for 897 (100.0%).
The lexicon it votes over is the same one the generator draws dishes from, so this is a check of the plumbing, not a cuisine classifier.
